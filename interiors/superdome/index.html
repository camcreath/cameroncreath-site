Superdome Clubs + Grab & Go Markets

Private clubs and grab-and-go markets for the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans.

Role

Junior designer

Studio

Gensler Los Angeles, hospitality

Where

Caesars Superdome, New Orleans

I did

Design · finish + furniture selection · graphics + vendor coordination · presentations · drawings

Two kinds of space in one stadium.

The Gensler LA hospitality studio designed the private clubs. I was a junior designer on the team. Then I was given the grab-and-go markets to design myself.

Finishes and furniture

Legends Club base finishes: warm wood, black marble, brass. I selected finishes and furniture and coordinated with vendors through install.

A kit of parts

My design director gave me room to experiment. The result: one kit of counters, shelving and signs that works for every grab-and-go space in the building.
